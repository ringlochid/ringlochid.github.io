Changelog

0.15.1

10 September 2026.

  • Keep switcher close buttons and their hover and focus states inside the card header.
  • Show skipped-tab counts when closing tabs, with Details explaining the reason.
  • Recover from temporary loading failures and offer Retry when the library or switcher cannot refresh.
  • Remove the name and destination suggestion after dragging tabs into a new collection.

0.15.0

9 September 2026.

  • Get started with a skippable guide, six offline video demonstrations, theme choices and optional import. Reopen it from Settings or Help.
  • Drag tabs, selections and whole groups into empty collections with clear insertion feedback, copy support and Undo.
  • Place groups before, between or after independent tabs, preserving that order when reopening collections and restoring backups.
  • Keep dialog buttons and themes consistent throughout the library and switcher.
  • Offer optional email feedback after uninstalling and explain which local data is removed.

0.14.0

9 September 2026.

  • Search across all spaces using collection cards. Choosing a space clears search; Clear search returns to the previous view.
  • Close tabs and groups in the switcher with Alt+W or the group close button, with Undo and keyboard focus preserved.
  • Keep existing groups stable during navigation and manually ungrouped tabs independent.
  • Align AI grouping across collections, open tabs and the switcher, with independent tabs before groups.
  • Fix text selection during renaming, switcher rename alignment, and long titles overflowing search results.

0.13.1

Available in the Chrome Web Store from 9 September 2026.

  • Save tabs into collections with groups and notes, organised into spaces.
  • Switch projects, search saved links and open tabs, and use the visual tab switcher.
  • Undo supported changes and recover saved work from Timeline and collection history.
  • Export backup JSON, bookmark HTML or Markdown, with optional AI and Notion connections.
